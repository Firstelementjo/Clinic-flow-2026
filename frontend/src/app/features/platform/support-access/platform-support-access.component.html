Strict Zero-Knowledge & Read-Only Policy

Platform operators have NO patient data access by default. To investigate production issues, operators must request a time-limited grant with a written justification. Break-glass grants auto-expire within a maximum of 120 minutes, enforce strictly read-only mode (all clinical mutations are blocked with HTTP 403), notify the clinic admin in tamper-evident logs, and are fully audited.
